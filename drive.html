Straight answers

What's included, what's not

The car — included

A Tesla Model 3 or Model Y from our fleet, charged and cleaned when you get it. It stays a Sophic car; you don't take on a loan or a lease.

Insurance — included

You're covered under Sophic's commercial policy while driving our car. No need to buy a rideshare endorsement yourself.

Maintenance — included

Service, software and mechanical repairs from normal use are on us. If the car goes down we work to get you into another one.

Charging — on you

About $20 per 500 miles across our fleet, tracked per car through Tesla's fleet system and billed automatically. A fraction of gas.

The split — 50% until target

Each week, half of your earnings go toward the car until the weekly target is met. Past that, every dollar is yours. Big weeks pay you, not us.

Your status — contractor

You're an independent contractor driving on your own platform accounts, on your own schedule. We don't set your hours.
