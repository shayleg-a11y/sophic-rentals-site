Questions

Good to know

Model 3 is $500/week, Model Y $600, Model X $700. At pickup you pay your first week plus a refundable deposit equal to your insurance policy's deductible — with a $500 deductible that's $1,000 total for a Model 3, minus the $9.99 verification fee you already paid. A lower deductible means a lower deposit. After that it's the weekly rent plus charging (about $20 per 500 miles, billed automatically). Insurance is your own policy. Minimum term is one month.

Checking availability is free. The $9.99 is paid only when you finish your application, covers verifying your license and insurance, and is credited toward your first week's rent at pickup. If you're not approved it isn't refunded. Full policy.

Your own auto policy with full coverage — liability plus comprehensive and collision — that extends to a rental vehicle, with Sophic Rentals listed as an additional interest so we're notified if it lapses. If you drive rideshare, tell your carrier; most add rideshare coverage for a few dollars a month. We verify with your carrier before pickup and re-check monthly. Don't have it yet? Read our get-insured-in-24-hours guide and apply anyway.

21 or older, a valid U.S. driver's license that isn't suspended, and full-coverage insurance as above. No credit check. Cars stay in California.

Most drivers are verified within 24–48 hours of uploading their license. Then you pick up at whichever of our three locations has your car — often the same or next day.

One of three offices: near LAX, near John Wayne Airport (SNA), or next to Chapman University in Orange. We assign the location based on where your car is and text you the address once you're approved. You only come in once — the car stays with you.

Charge anywhere — Superchargers, home, work. Our cars run on Tesla's fleet management, so charging is tracked per car and billed to you automatically with your weekly rent. Across the fleet it averages about $20 per 500 miles.

5,000 miles per week is included, which covers even full-time driving. Over that is $1 per mile.

Scheduled service, software and mechanical repairs from normal use are on us, and if a car has to come off the road we work to get you into another one. Wear-and-tear items you consume — tires, brake pads, wipers — are the driver's responsibility.
