Verification fee

Starting an application is free. To be verified, you pay a one-time verification fee of $9.99 through Stripe, which pays for checking your license, driving record and insurance. For rental customers the $9.99 is credited toward your first week's rent when you pick up. It is not otherwise refundable — including if your application is declined, if you change your mind, or if a vehicle you preferred is no longer available. It is not a deposit. Full deposit and approval policies are on the policies page.

Who can apply

  • You must be 21 or older and hold a valid, unsuspended U.S. driver's license (California for fleet drivers).
  • Rental customers must carry their own full-coverage auto insurance — liability plus comprehensive and collision — that extends to a rental vehicle, with Sophic Rentals listed as an additional interest, and must disclose rideshare use to their carrier. Proof is required before pickup and the policy must stay active for the entire term.
  • We verify what you tell us. Giving false information, or uploading a license that isn't yours, ends the application and forfeits the fee.
  • Approval is at our discretion. Vehicle availability is shown in good faith but changes daily.

Fleet-driver program summary

Fleet drivers are independent contractors, not employees, and drive on their own platform accounts and schedule. Each week 50% of platform earnings are paid to Sophic until the weekly target in your Driver Agreement is met; earnings above the target are yours. The vehicle, commercial insurance while driving it, and maintenance are provided by Sophic; charging is paid by the driver. The target, mileage and personal-use terms are set in writing in the Driver Agreement before you start.
