How to get rideshare-ready insurance in 24 hours

Insurance is the step where most drivers get stuck. Here's exactly what we need, what to ask for, and who sells it — so you can be verified the same week you apply.

What we need before pickup

  • Full coverage on your own auto policy: liability plus comprehensive and collision.
  • Coverage that extends to a rental / non-owned car — ask your agent to confirm it in writing.
  • A rideshare (TNC) endorsement if you'll drive Uber, Lyft or delivery. Personal policies usually exclude app driving without it.
  • Sophic Rentals listed as "Additional Interest" (also called "Interested Party"). It's free and doesn't change your coverage — it just lets your insurer tell us if the policy lapses.

Your 24-hour checklist

  1. Hour 0 — apply free on this site so we can hold a car while you sort insurance.
  2. Hour 1 — get 2–3 quotes. Tell each carrier: "I'm renting a Tesla Model 3 long-term and driving Uber/Lyft. I need full coverage that applies to a rented car, with a rideshare endorsement."
  3. Hour 2 — buy the policy (most carriers can bind same day online or by phone).

  5. Hour 4 — send us your declarations page (screenshot is fine). We call your carrier to confirm, usually same or next business day.

Who sells rideshare endorsements in California

Many major carriers sell a rideshare add-on to a personal policy in California, including State Farm, Farmers, Progressive, Clearcover and USAA (USAA is for military families). GEICO is also a carrier our renters have used. Availability and price change, so get quotes from at least two. Independent insurance agents can shop several carriers in one call.

Common snags (and fixes)

  • "We don't cover rentals." Ask specifically about coverage for a non-owned or long-term rented vehicle; if it's a no, quote another carrier.
  • Liability-only today? Call your current carrier and ask to add comprehensive and collision plus the rideshare endorsement — often a same-day change.
  • No car on your policy right now? Tell the carrier the car you'll be driving; we can give you the VIN once you're matched.
  • Out-of-state license? You can still apply; some carriers will ask for a California license within a set period.
