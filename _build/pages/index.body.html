At pickup you pay your first week plus a refundable deposit equal to your insurance policy's deductible — with a $500 deductible that's $1,000 total for a Model 3, minus the $9.99 verification fee you already paid. A lower deductible means a lower deposit. After that it's the weekly rent plus charging (about $20 per 500 miles, billed automatically). Insurance is your own policy. Minimum term is one month.

Every application (rent to own or rental) has a one-time $9.99 fee. It covers verifying your license and insurance, and is credited toward your first weekly payment at pickup. If you're not approved it isn't refunded. Full policy.

Your own auto policy with full coverage — liability plus comprehensive and collision — that extends to a rental vehicle, with Sophic Rentals listed as an additional interest so we're notified if it lapses. If you drive rideshare, tell your carrier; most add rideshare coverage for a few dollars a month. We verify with your carrier before pickup and re-check monthly. Don't have it yet?
